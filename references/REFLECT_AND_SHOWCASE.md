# Reflect and Showcase

Use at MVP completion or another meaningful project milestone.

The purpose is not to generate marketing copy from memory. It is to synthesize accumulated repository evidence, daily logs, decisions, tests, prototypes, and handoffs into two different artifacts.

## 1. Learning Retrospective

Answer: **What can the learner now understand and do that they could not do before this project?**

Include:

- concepts learned;
- concepts personally practiced;
- concepts understood but delegated;
- architecture and system-thinking patterns learned;
- debugging patterns encountered;
- technical decision-making patterns;
- important misconceptions corrected;
- how the learner's mental model evolved;
- what the learner can now do independently;
- remaining knowledge gaps;
- recommended next learning direction.

Prefer concrete evidence over lists of technology names.

Distinguish:

- exposure;
- conceptual understanding;
- hands-on practice;
- independent capability.

Suggested output: `docs/LEARNING_RETROSPECTIVE.md`.

## 2. Portfolio Case Study

Answer: **What does this project credibly demonstrate to another person?**

Build the narrative from evidence:

1. Problem / opportunity
2. Project goal and constraints
3. Initial hypothesis
4. Architecture / approach
5. Key product and technical decisions
6. Important tradeoffs and rejected alternatives
7. Build evolution
8. Technical challenges and debugging
9. Human--AI / human--agent collaboration where relevant
10. Outcome and working MVP
11. Evidence: prototypes, screenshots, commits, tests, observations, metrics if real
12. Reflection and what would be changed next

For AI or agentic products, explicitly inspect accumulated evidence for:

- initiative vs user control;
- explainability;
- uncertainty;
- confirmation and override;
- context construction;
- failure recovery;
- trust;
- division of responsibility between human and AI.

Do not claim user research, metrics, production impact, or causality that was not actually observed.

Suggested output: `docs/CASE_STUDY.md`.

## 3. Showcase Gap Analysis

Before declaring the case study ready, identify gaps between:

- what was built;
- what was learned;
- what can actually be demonstrated;
- what the desired portfolio story requires.

Recommend only high-value finishing work. Do not automatically turn the MVP into a production-polish project.

Possible finishing work may include:

- one missing interaction that completes the story;
- clearer before/after evidence;
- a small evaluation;
- a diagram explaining architecture;
- a short demo;
- cleanup needed for credibility;
- explicit documentation of a key tradeoff.

## Principle

**Reflection converts experience into learning. Showcase synthesis converts evidence into proof.**

Keep these outputs separate even when they draw from the same project history.
